### Release 4.4.2:

- **Emails in thermo's code.** thermo writes all 120 marketplace emails on one shared layout; SendGrid only delivers them, and the ten old templates are no longer read.
- **Emails after the save.** A refused send no longer undoes a sign-up, a deployment or a ticket update, and an action that rolls back sends nothing.
- **Failed sends say so.** When a verification, invitation or new-address confirmation can't be sent, the action stays saved and the screen says so; forgot password gives the same answer for every address.
- **The right recipients.** People who can't sign in get no ticket, build, trial or listing emails, staff emails meant for them go to admin@, and a ticket update isn't emailed to the person who made it.
- **Trials, stuck purchases, refunds.** The "trial ended" email goes once per end date; a stuck AI Assistant purchase emails staff once, then weekly, and ends once its charge is refunded or voided in Braintree; a customer whose purchase failed after the charge hears how it was refunded.
- **Receipts, Service Agreement, Help form.** The receipt names the deployment and billing period and carries the full address; the Service Agreement names the client; the Help form takes 5 questions an hour and 20 a day.
- **Deploy order.** ozone 4_4_2 first, check its four columns, then thermo straight after.
