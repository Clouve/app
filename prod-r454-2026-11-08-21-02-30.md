### Release 4.5.4:

- **Only admins pay (T10).** Client users see "Only <organization>'s admins can complete purchases." where they used to see Subscribe or a paid change, and thermo refuses those purchases too.
- **AI Assistant not ready (T9).** A paid deployment whose AI key can't be made says "The AI Assistant isn't available yet."
- **Upgrade (T15).** Upgrade is off, with the reason under it, when a listing has no upgrade steps; thermo and strato refuse it before anything stops.
- **Sign-in pages (L1).** The grey cover and the big spinner are gone; the button keeps its own spinner.
- **Password changes (G4).** A changed AI Assistant password no longer locks its owner out.
- **Clones into another organization (R13).** The clone's AI Assistant starts with an empty home, without the source's Claude sign-in or chat history.
- **Turn-on after a clone (U2).** The clone's build no longer undoes it.
- **Cancel email (E1).** The payer's copy of a whole refund says the subscription was canceled, as the admins' copy does.
- **Failed backups (R1).** Their records leave the Backups tab. Restore shows only on backups that can be restored, and it checks the backup before it deletes anything.
- **SSO and custom domains (R2).** SSO keeps a custom domain's certificate.
- **AI-off launches (R12).** The AI Assistant's volumes wait until it's turned on, once Velero has Clouve's volume policy.
- **Screenshots (S1).** They must be the listing organization's own uploads.
- **Container rules (T11, T12).** An install-time value can't name a container, and an image on Clouve's registry must come from the organization's own project.
- **AI Assistant refusals (G5).** They start "AI Assistant: " and open the step that holds the field.
- **Alias icons (T13).** They survive a compose download and upload.
- **Published By (P1).** The panel follows the organization's current name, logo and website. Signed-out visitors see the logo from a CDN copy that the listing's next stage makes.
- **Caches (T8).** A publish warms the marketplace's public pages and sitemap.
- **ConfigMap values (F4).** A value whose first line starts with a space no longer breaks the deploy.
- **Clusters page (C1).** Finished deletes list as Deleted, last. A delete whose result got lost heals by itself, and a script settles the old rows.
- **Robot secrets (R5).** A Super Admin rotates a cluster's robot secrets from its Actions menu.
- **Client Details (D1).** It is one page: the facts first, then People.
- **thermo's Harbor login (H1).** thermo signs in to Harbor with its own robot. The admin sign-in limit is built and stays off until 4.5.5.
- **Token action.** It makes only four objects, in the deployment's own namespace, for the signed-in user. That closes an older hole.
- **Secrets in actions (F1, F2).** strato keeps them off command lines and out of Harbor's admin Job log, and the deploy actions' environment no longer carries them.
- **Stateful pods (U1).** GKE's autoscaler can't evict them mid-backup or mid-push.
- **Cross-tenant checks (G1, G2, R1-S, S-c).** thermo and strato check Repair's robot copy, a restore's sign-in, backup names and the copy source.
- **Ticket JSONs and old folders (T5, T16).** Tickets hold only their own columns, and old listing folders get a count and a delete tool.
- **Logs and OIDC (F3, T6).** thermo logs no typed email address, and production thermo won't start without its OIDC client secret.
- **Secret readers (T7).** The Dockerfile readers read more forms, and iono's npm test runs its node tests.
- **Build failure alert (T9).** A failed AI Assistant build logs one line an alert matches.
- Full notes: releases/RELEASE_NOTES_v4.5.4.md in the docs repo.
