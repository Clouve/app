### Release 4.4.4:

- **The page scrolls past the AI Assistant.** With the pointer over the assistant, the wheel scrolls the assistant while it can and then the page, also while the assistant is busy.
- **Staging and publishing show their progress.** The bar follows thermo's steps and percent, with "file n of m" while files copy. The publish bar sits inside the Approve & Publish dialog, which can't be closed while it runs and keeps what the reviewer typed if it fails. Approve & Publish turns on as soon as a stage finishes.
- **One search box on every list.** The same box everywhere, with a clear button, Esc to clear, Enter to search at once, a count, and a line that says when nothing matches.
- **Clients and Tickets.** Both lists are redesigned and are searched, sorted and paged by thermo. Super admins get a Tickets page.
- **Fixes.** An AI listing with no skills URL can be staged again, an empty "enabled" counts as enabled, compact marketplace rows fit a phone, and the deploy picker no longer shows marketplace buttons that can't work there.
- **Deploy order.** thermo, then meso; iono any time; no ozone release. Then the magneto-agent image, and a Firestore TTL policy on operation-progress.expiresAt in each project.
