### Release 4.5.8:

- **A fast marketplace.** The console, the sign-in screens and the docs load only when someone opens them, reCAPTCHA loads only on the account screens, and files go out compressed. Mobile Lighthouse on /marketplace went from 39 to 91 on the rig.
- **Grouped by job.** The marketplace's first screen says what it is and groups listings by the buyer's job: School, Business operations, Websites, Files and sharing, Surveys and forms, Developer tools, and AI last.
- **Listing pages that sell.** Each listing gets its own title and opening sentence, the price and trial in its description, and structured data search engines read. /marketplace lists them the same way, and /llms.txt describes them for AI search.
- **Sign-up keeps the app.** A person who signs up from a listing lands on its install form, through the verification email too. "Sign up with Google" is on the sign-up page, with "By signing up with Google, you agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy." and both links.
- **Phone numbers.** The phone number is optional and takes international numbers.
- **Developer sign-up.** Developers sign up on /signup?developer=true, with Google too.
- **Plainer listing texts.** Every house listing gets plainer texts once each environment's sitting applies them. A new listing version makes Upgrade available on that listing's deployments; nothing changes unless someone presses it.
- **Staff cookie.** Staff sign-ins set clv_staff=1 on .clouve.com, never for a client, so GA4 can mark staff traffic on www too.
- **New categories.** The publish wizard has four more: Business Operations, Websites, Files and Sharing, and Surveys and Forms.
- **Investor deck.** It stays at its address but leaves search.
- **thermo before meso.** meso 4.5.8 sends fields thermo 4.5.7 refuses, so thermo always ships first.
- **Build checks.** meso's build fails when console or sign-in code joins the marketplace's first download or a source map is made; nginx serves brotli and gzip copies with a year-long cache on hashed files.
- **Vite 8.1.7.** meso moves from Vite 8.1.6 to 8.1.7.
- **Tokens out of the address.** A first script in meso's page moves reset and invitation tokens out of the address before GTM or anything else can read them.
- **The rig.** The rig's meso runs only as a production build, in devenv's manifest as well as on the rig.

Full notes: releases/RELEASE_NOTES_v4.5.8.md in the docs repo.
