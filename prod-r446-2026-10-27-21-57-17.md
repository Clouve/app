### Release 4.4.6:

- **Uptime checks for every running app.** On prod, each public host of a running deployment gets a Cloud Monitoring check every 5 minutes from three US regions: the app host, a bundle's other apps, the AI Assistant's host while it's on, and custom domains that point at the cluster. thermo adds them after a successful build, removes them on pause or delete, and reconciles them once a day.
- **A deep health route.** GET /thermo/health answers ok or fail for the database, Firestore and Pub/Sub, so the platform check watches more than the process answering.
- **Stuck deployment tickets are reported.** A job every 10 minutes logs each deployment ticket that has been in progress for more than 30 minutes, once per stuck spell, and the platform alerts pick it up.
- **One email when the daily status check finds a deployment broken.** The admin mailbox gets a single "Failed: status on" email when the unattended check turns a resolved ticket open. Later failing days send nothing.
- **Custom domains.** The domain check gives up after 5 seconds and says it couldn't look the domain up, instead of hanging.
- **Deploy order.** thermo, meso and iono in any order; meso and iono are version only. Before prod: thermo's monitoring settings on, and thermo's account granted its uptime-check role.
