### Release 4.4.5:

- **Clients rate a support ticket to close it.** When Clouve marks a support ticket resolved, the client sees "Is your problem solved?". Yes leads to a rating from 1 to 5 and an optional comment, and the ticket closes. No puts it back to Open with the client's note. The resolved email has a "Rate and close the ticket" button.
- **Only a super admin closes a ticket without a rating.** Staff can no longer close a support ticket from its status or change its type. A super admin can close one with a reason, which the client reads in the comments. A closed support ticket stays closed.
- **Ratings where staff work.** The rating shows on the ticket and in the assignee's email. A 1 or 2 also emails the client's first-tier agent and the admin mailbox. The staff Tickets list gets a Rating column, a Rating filter and the average in its opening sentence.
- **A Satisfaction page.** Super admins see every client and first-tier agents their own: ratings by month, by expert and by client, and what clients wrote.
- **Footer.** n8n is no longer on the marketing footer's trademark line.
- **Deploy order.** ozone 4_4_5 first (six new ticket columns), then thermo, then meso. iono is version only.
