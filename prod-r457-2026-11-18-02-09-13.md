### Release 4.5.7:

- **Keep me signed in.** A box on the sign-in page: ticked, a sign-in lasts 7 days on that device; unticked, 12 hours. It works for Google and for Clouve's own form.
- **New tabs.** A new tab, or the browser opened again, lands signed in while the sign-in lasts. Signing out also signs out the browser's other Clouve tabs.
- **AI Assistant sign-in.** The AI Assistant keeps its sign-in through an agent restart, within the same 24-hour and 7-day limits.
- **Titles after an Upgrade.** The deployment's title and its confirm dialogs name the new version.
- **Refused Upgrades.** An Upgrade Clouve refuses before it changes anything shows a calm notice on the deployment page, and the client gets no email about it.
- **Client emails.** Clouve can email a client's people about service news, from "Clouve support" or from a staff member by name, with an optional announcement above every page until a date.
- **Messages.** A Messages page under Client Care and "Send a message to N clients" on the Clients list: Super Admins and sales agents email any clients, FTAs their own, with a review of who gets it, a test email to themselves and an announcement they can end.
- **Headless Services.** Upgrade can move a deployment whose Services had a cluster IP onto a version that makes them headless, which every version with the AI Assistant does.
- **Hand-added hosts.** An Upgrade keeps host labels staff added by hand and serves their hosts again once the new version is up.
- **Refusal emails.** A refused Upgrade emails only the deployment's agent, saying nothing changed and the client wasn't emailed.
- **Robot secrets.** Rotate robot secrets works again on a registered cluster set up with 4.5.6's access, and so do restores and relaunches there.
- **Clone patches.** A clone's one-shot patches retry, and a write that still fails names kubectl's reason.
- **Upgrade exit status.** strato's upgrade action ends with 65 when it stops before the pause, 1 for a failure after it, and 0 when it worked.
- **Title script.** scripts/upgrade-titles.js renames tickets upgraded before 4.5.7 (count, dry run, write; ids and counts only).
- **Long sessions.** scripts/end-long-sessions.js ends sessions longer than 12 hours, a step of any thermo rollback below 4.5.7.
- **Kept sign-ins file.** The AI Assistant keeps its sign-ins in /var/lib/clouve/auth/sessions.json on the agent's disk: SHA-256 keys only, owner only, bound to the deployment.

Full notes: releases/RELEASE_NOTES_v4.5.7.md in the docs repo.
