### Release 4.4.3:

- **Sign-up keeps accounts private.** Every email sign-up ends on the same page; an address that already has an account gets a "You already have a Clouve account" email instead, and nothing is created.
- **Sign-in gives one answer.** An unknown address, a wrong password and an account with no password hear the same sentence; a turned-off account is told so only after the right password.
- **Turned-off people.** They get no reset link and no role or details email, an old reset or invitation link stops working, and a turned-off first-tier agent can't be given clients.
- **When reCAPTCHA doesn't answer.** Sign-up, sign-in, reset password and Forgot password say so after 10 seconds and keep what was typed.
- **AI Assistant purchases.** A new daily job checks every unfinished purchase, even on paused or deleted deployments; it refunds the rest after a partial hand refund and never reads a duplicate as a decline.
- **Emails.** No SendGrid Unsubscribe link; build emails skip the person who ran an action that worked, except an install, a copy or a delete; one copy of a ticket email per person; staff footers name the environment.
- **Tickets and partners.** The estimate rule and field values hold for every API call, updateTicketState works, customers no longer see a rejected estimate in history, and partners see a warning when an invitation wasn't sent.
- **Deploy order.** thermo, then meso; iono any time; no ozone release. Pause the new AI check job and its warmup once on dev and UAT; before prod, run the read-only list in the operational notes.
Full notes: releases/RELEASE_NOTES_v4.4.3.md in the docs repo.
